Every message a thread sends

A thread messages its home only when the home has something to do. Nothing is sent when a pull request opens, when a review is written, or when its findings are handled.

Whom a message goes to

The id in a home-id, read when the message is sent: the root home's, or the one in <scope>/ when the Home: line of the brief or request names that scope. No brief names a thread. With no home listening, nothing is sent and the next home reads the result from disk.

The root home and a scoped home

A repo has one root home, and may have scoped homes beside it, each for one goal. Briefs, requests and notes are shared. A handoff, a plan and the gates are a home's own.

~/.local/state/home/<repo>/

The repo's directory, shared by every home of the repo

notes.mdtasks/<slug>.mdscouts/<slug>.mdreports/merge.logoff

./

The root home's own. Started with /home, or by the hook.

handoff.mdplan.mdgates.mdhome-threadlog/

<scope>/

A scoped home's own. Started with /home <scope>.

handoff.mdplan.mdgates.mdhome-threadlog/
  • Only the root home makes a scope, and only on your word.
  • No home writes another home's directory.
  • A scoped home's slugs and branches start with its prefix.
  • Each home merges its own pull requests. jmerge --all takes the others too, on your word.
  • Homes do not message each other, but for a decision that crosses scopes.
  • No home asks another what it has out: jmerge --claims prints it.
  • A scoped home whose plan has no open entry closes at its handoff: no next home is started.
  • The root home reads a scope's handoff and open plan entries when you ask for status.

jmerge: the merge, with no model

One command does the merge, the sweep and the launch check. The home runs it and relays what it printed.

What "ready" and "removable" mean

Ready: a review is written, its findings are handled (Fixed:), a wider fix has had its second pass, the check named check has passed at its head, that check covers today's main, and GitHub says the pull request can merge. The rules are in features/jmerge/src/jmerge/ready.py. Removable: the last Result says done with nothing left, the pull request is merged or there is none, and nothing uncommitted or unpushed would be lost. The rules are in features/jmerge/src/jmerge/sweep.py.

A thread may settle when everything it knows is on disk and nothing unmerged or uncommitted depends on it. Settling a thread leaves its worktree and branch: those are the merge's and the sweep's.
