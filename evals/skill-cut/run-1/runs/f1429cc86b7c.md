### Act On

**1. Step 5's catch-up conflicts with "One tick" step 4 (A1, B1)**
- **Where:** `playbooks/autopilot-full.md:19`.
- **Problem:** Ketchup covers everything "since the last message they wrote". In autopilot the user is away for hours. So every tick that changed something would repeat earlier ticks' news. That breaks One tick's "Nothing that an earlier message said".
- **Why act:** This is a concrete, hourly execution path, and both reviewers found it independently. As written, the home either produces a growing reply or ignores the new sentence. Either way the Done-when line isn't really met.
- **Fix inside Where:** Bound the catch-up in step 5 to what this tick changed and nothing an earlier message said, in ketchup's voice.
- **Later, outside Where:** Moving the rule into One tick step 4 (`skills/home/queue-and-its-hourly-audit.md`) would remove the duplicate.

**2. The Hand over lines require content that ketchup tells the writer to cut (A3, B2)**
- **Where:** `playbooks/autopilot-full.md:32` and `playbooks/autopilot-stack.md:33`.
- **Problem:** Each entry needs its link, state, PR and commit. Ketchup says to stay under 150 words, leave out hashes, and cut "what happened" first. A real queue can't satisfy both.
- **Why act:** Both reviewers raised it, and the conflict is concrete. The likely failure is the worst one: the per-entry account that step 7 requires gets trimmed.
- **Fix:** Say which wins. The per-entry account, as step 7 says, should be kept whole and exempt from the word limit and the no-hash rule. Ketchup then governs the voice, the opening and the action items.

**3. "Changed something *for the user*" leaves ticks that fit neither ending (A2, B3)**
- **Where:** `playbooks/autopilot-full.md:19`.
- **Problem:** A tick that only launched a crewmate or re-sent a stuck thread fits neither sentence. Ketchup's "leave out work that changed nothing for the user" pushes it toward silence. One tick explicitly requires reporting those.
- **Why act:** The Done-when line splits ticks into exactly two cases, and the qualifier breaks that split. The fix is one phrase: drop "for the user".
- **Caveat:** If 8043881 uses that exact phrase, the drift diff will show it. In that case, keep jstack's local form and note why, rather than silently diverging.
