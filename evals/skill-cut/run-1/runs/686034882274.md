First I map the reading's severities to the section's, as step 4, "Lead judgment", says: `critical` becomes `error`, and `nit` becomes `info`.

I write each finding as one line in the form `<severity> <file>:<line> <what is wrong> (principle: <name>)`. For example:
`error src/foo.sh:42 <what is wrong> (principle: <name>)` and `info src/bar.sh:17 <what is wrong> (principle: none)`.

Before I keep the nit at `info`, I check it: a crewmate declines `info` by default, so if it must be fixed in this pull request I rate it `warning` instead.

Next, from "After the review", step 2 ("Outside the diff"), I put both lines into the `FINDINGS` heredoc of `gh pr diff <url> | awk '…' - pass=2 /dev/fd/3 3<<'FINDINGS'`. I keep only the lines it prints and put its count on the `Dropped:` line.
