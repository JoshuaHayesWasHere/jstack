First, map the reading's severities onto the section's three words, as "The review", step 4 ("Lead judgment") says: the reading's `critical` becomes `error`, and its `nit` becomes `info`.

Before writing the `info` line, I check one thing. A crewmate declines an `info` finding by default, so if the nit must be fixed in this pull request, I rate it `warning` instead.

Then I write each finding in the form "Outside the diff" gives, `<severity> <file>:<line> <what is wrong> (principle: <name>)`, for example:
`error src/x.sh:42 <what is wrong> (principle: <name>)`
`info src/y.sh:7 <what is wrong> (principle: none)`

These lines go into the `FINDINGS` heredoc of the "Outside the diff" `gh pr diff <url> | awk …` block, which keeps or drops them before anything is sent or recorded.
