First I map the reading's severities to the section's three words, as "The review", step 4 ("Lead judgment") says: `critical` is written `error`, and `nit` is written `info`.

I write each kept Act on/Consider finding as one line in the form `<severity> <file>:<line> <what is wrong> (principle: <name>)`, for example:
`error <file>:<line> <what is wrong> (principle: <name>)`
`info <file>:<line> <what is wrong> (principle: <name>)`

If the nit must be fixed in this pull request, I rate it `warning` instead, because a crewmate declines `info` findings by default. These lines then go through the "Outside the diff" block (After the review, step 2) before they are sent or recorded.
