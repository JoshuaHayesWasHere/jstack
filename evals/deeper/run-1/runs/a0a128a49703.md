# check-slowness: find what slows down `make check` and why

Task: "figure out what is slowing down make check and why"
Done when:
- Each step `make check` runs has been timed on a clean run, and the timings are written down.
- The step or steps that take most of the time are named, with their measured share of the total.
- For each slow step, the cause is explained and backed by evidence such as a profile, a timing comparison or a log excerpt.
- The findings are reported back, along with candidate fixes, without applying any of those fixes.

Where: the `Makefile` `check` target and everything it calls (lint, type-check and test commands, their configs, and the test suite under the Python commands)
Do not touch: any file in the repo. This is investigation only, so do not change the `Makefile`, the tests, the configs or the dependencies.
Decisions: none
Design: none, it is an investigation that produces findings rather than a change to design
Pull request: ask first
Review: none
