The modes run these on their own. You can also start the two skills by typing their names.

| Item | Starts | Does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Playbooks | A mode picks one | Step lists for a kind of task, such as Bug fix or Feature. See [[Playbooks]]. | pstack, adapted |
| Principles | A mode reads one | Short rules that a mode cites when a rule shaped a decision. See [[Principles]]. | pstack |
| `blast-radius` | Typed | Finds what a small change could break outside the diff. Proves the one fact that makes it safe by running code. | pstack |
| `show-me-your-work` | Typed | Keeps a TSV log with one row per decision on long or unattended runs. | pstack |
